CONTRIBUTING.md

How to merge

Mona pushes the paddle. Bounce the ball into the contribution-graph squares. Darker commits need more hits. Break every commit to merge the pull request and move on to the next repository. Merge conflicts are unbreakable and are not required.

Keyboard

← → or A D move. Space launches. P or Esc pauses. M mutes all audio.

Mouse and touch

Move the mouse or drag across the board to steer. Click or tap to launch.

Power-ups

  • Copilot: two extra balls and 10 seconds of auto-aim toward the nearest commit.
  • Dependabot: a 15-second shield saves one dropped ball and patches bugs you catch.
  • Actions: 8 seconds of automatic lasers that break commits and zap bugs.
  • Bugs fall from bug bricks. Catching one without a shield shortens the paddle for 8 seconds.

You have three lives. Your best score is saved in this browser. The game pauses when it loses focus.
